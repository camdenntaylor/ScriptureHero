# Circle cover artwork

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Final project asset: `public/images/circle-covers.webp` (1536 × 1024). The PNG output was encoded as WebP with ffmpeg at quality 82. CSS crops the atlas into six decorative covers; no reference mockup pixels are shipped.

Grid: olive branches, mountains, tea by a window; garden, woodland path, ocean. The card crop uses the middle third of each square tile; membership thumbnails show the complete square. All assets are served locally and retain their natural colors in evening mode.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: one reusable photographic cover atlas for community circle cards on a quiet scripture website. Create a single landscape-format image with EXACTLY 3 equal columns and 2 equal rows, six photographic panels touching edge-to-edge with NO gutters, NO borders, NO text, NO labels. Every panel is a separate natural editorial photograph with warm soft sunlight, realistic texture, forest greens, ivory and honey tones. Top-left: closeup sunlit olive branches and golden bokeh. Top-middle: layered mountain ridges at soft peach sunrise. Top-right: ceramic tea mug beside a sunlit window with a neutral knitted blanket and garden beyond. Bottom-left: warm sunlight over a quiet garden with wildflowers. Bottom-middle: gently winding woodland walking path under leafy trees with dappled afternoon light. Bottom-right: calm ocean horizon at peach sunrise. All six tiles must be precisely equal size on the 3x2 grid, with clean aligned cuts. Each photo is composed to remain useful as a wide shallow crop; key subjects centered. This is production artwork, not a website mockup. No people, symbols, logos, words, or watermark. Output an overall 3:2 landscape composition.
